## Name
Downly – Download Manager Integration

## Short description (≤132 chars)
EN: Send downloads, videos and playlists from your browser to the Downly app for faster, resumable downloads.

## Full description (EN)
Downly connects your browser to the Downly desktop download manager.

• Takes over your browser downloads and speeds them up with multiple connections
• Pause and resume downloads, even after restarting your PC
• A Download button on videos, which you can drag anywhere or hide
• Save video or audio only (MP3, M4A…) in the quality you choose
• Download whole playlists
• Right-click any link → Download with Downly
• Download all links on a page at once
• Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+D (video), Alt+Shift+A (audio)
• English and Arabic

Requires the free Downly app for Windows / macOS / Linux: https://downly.vercel.app

## Permission justifications (Chrome "Privacy practices" tab)
- downloads: to hand browser downloads over to the Downly app.
- contextMenus: the "Download with Downly" right-click items.
- cookies: pass the site's session to the app so logged-in downloads work.
- storage: save the user's settings (language, button position).
- webRequest: find video/audio files the page loads to list them in the popup.
- scripting: collect links for "Download all links on this page".
- host permissions (<all_urls>): show the download button on videos and detect media on any site the user visits.
- Single purpose: send downloads and media from the browser to the Downly download manager.
