# Downly Browser Extension — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

The Downly extension connects your browser to the Downly desktop app running on your own computer.

## What the extension accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| URLs of downloads you start, links you right-click, and the page you're on when you press a Downly button | So the Downly app can download them | Only to the Downly app on your computer (`127.0.0.1`) |
| Cookies for the site of a file being downloaded | Some sites only allow logged-in downloads; the app needs the same session | Only to the Downly app on your computer |
| Addresses of video/audio files a page loads | To list "Files found on this page" in the popup | Kept in your browser's session storage, cleared when the tab closes |
| Your extension settings (language, button position, sites where the button is hidden) | To remember your choices | Stored locally in your browser |

## What we don't do

- We do **not** send any of this data to our servers or to any third party.
- We do **not** track your browsing, show ads, or sell data.
- The extension has no analytics.

The desktop app contacts only the websites you choose to download from, and GitHub (to update its download engine).
