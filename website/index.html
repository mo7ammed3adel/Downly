FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free with no limits, no ads and no account. Donations are optional.

Is it safe?

Downly doesn’t collect your data or show ads. The browser extension only talks to the app on your own computer.

How does Spotify work?

Downly reads the song details from Spotify, finds the same song on YouTube and saves it with Spotify’s title, artist and cover.

Does it work on Windows 7?

Yes. There’s a special build for Windows 7, 8 and 8.1 (32-bit and 64-bit) — the download button picks it automatically.

Windows says the app is unrecognized

New apps show this until they build reputation. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

Mac says Downly can't be opened

macOS does this for apps from outside the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.

Can I download anything?

Please only download content you have the right to download, and respect each site’s terms and copyright.
