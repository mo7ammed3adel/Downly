Downly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Downly (the desktop app and its browser extension) is built to keep your data on your own computer.

What the extension accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
URLs of downloads you start, links you right-click, and the page you're on when you press a Downly button So the Downly app can download them Only to the Downly app on your computer (127.0.0.1)
Cookies for the site of a file being downloaded Some sites only allow downloads while you're logged in Only to the Downly app on your computer
Addresses of video/audio files a page loads To list “Files found on this page” in the popup Your browser's session storage, cleared when the tab closes
Extension settings (language, button position, hidden sites) To remember your choices Stored locally in your browser

What we don't do

  • We do not send your data to our servers or to any third party.
  • We do not track your browsing, show ads, or sell data.
  • Neither the app nor the extension contains analytics.

Network connections made by the app

The app connects only to the websites you choose to download from, and to GitHub to update its download engine (yt-dlp).
